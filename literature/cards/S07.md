# S07 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Bennedsen, Christensen, Christensen, Yu & Zhang (2026), The Rough HAR Model, arXiv 2609.21587.

## Version and status

42 PDF pages; working paper/preprint. Author faculty page lists it as submitted. Published status not asserted.

## Inspected source locations

PDF pages 12-13 eqs 2.7-2.9 and footnotes 5-6; pp 17-18 ETF sample/forecast design; pp 22-24 Table 5 and explicit noise test eqs 4.1-4.2. p 23 visually inspected.

## Model and assumptions

Log-RAR is ARMA(1, 1); log-RHAR replaces AR1 with daily/5 day/22 day HAR regressors and MA1 innovation. Discrete-time roughness terminology is qualified by the authors themselves.

## Observation and error layer

Section 4 explicitly models observed log RV=y=x+w with independent noise. The latent AR is augmented by a genuine MA1 component; fixing noise variance 2/78=1/39 makes theta=0 a testable restriction (p 23).

## Estimator and target

Exact Gaussian maximum likelihood via Kalman filter; simulations map MA coefficient to fGn Wold coefficient. Empirical forecast window 500 days, refit daily, lognormal retransformation; source QLIKE is nonnegative ratio form (p 18).

## Data and horizon

Ten ETFs plus a 40 stock VOLARE robustness panel. SPY and sector ETFs, 5 minute RV; exact source fields and author code not acquired.

## Source-grounded finding

The authors report negative MA coefficients and forecast gains, with theta remaining around-0.4 under fixed measurement noise (p 23). This overlaps the initial project more closely than an accuracy-only description.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Known 2/m error variance is an approximation; latent noise and intrinsic MA are otherwise not separable from log RV dynamics alone. A discrete ARMA path does not identify a continuous-time local Holder exponent. HARQ underperformance alone is not a general structural identification test.

## Our replication status

No RAR/RHAR estimation or source table reproduction claimed. Exact equations audited; replication code not located on inspected author pages/fulltext.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Abandon the proposed novelty claim that this paper ignores measurement error. Focus on heterogeneous/correlated noise, matched covariance controls and separately measured decision sensitivity.
