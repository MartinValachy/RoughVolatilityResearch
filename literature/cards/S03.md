# S03 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Fukasawa, Takabatake & Westphal (2022), Consistent estimation for fractional stochastic volatility model under high-frequency asymptotics, Mathematical Finance 32(4), 1086-1132; DOI 10.1111/mafi.12354.

## Version and status

Downloaded Is Volatility Rough?, arXiv 1905.04852v 2 (2019), 51 PDF pages. This is a differently titled predecessor, not silently the 2022 journal text.

## Inspected source locations

PDF page 4 Figure 2; p 5 eqs 5-7 and Theorem 2.1; pp 6-7 local Gaussian/Whittle construction. p 5 visually inspected.

## Model and assumptions

d log(sigma_u^2)=kappa_u du+eta dW_u^H with adapted drift and H in a compact interior parameter set; business-time model freezes volatility while market closed.

## Observation and error layer

LogRV minus log integrated variance over a shrinking interval is modeled as independent, centered Gaussian error with variance 2/m. Theorem 2.1 motivates this under shrinking-day and increasing-within-day sampling asymptotics.

## Estimator and target

Noise-aware quasi-likelihood with a local Gaussian approximation and Whittle spectral approximation; a simple log-variogram slope is a preliminary diagnostic, not their final estimator.

## Data and horizon

Simulation and Oxford-Man index realized volatility. Figure 2 Brownian OU example uses d log sigma^2=10(-3.2-log sigma^2)du+0.8 dW independent of return Brownian motion.

## Source-grounded finding

Figure 2 reports progressively smaller apparentH when using coarser RV; their noise-aware empirical work supports roughness, not its universal removal (pp 4-5).

## Limitations and unresolved audit

The 2019 text says tests remain future work (p 4). A fixed 390 minute daily interval with strongly varying variance, correlated errors or price noise is not automatically covered by the local centered 2/m approximation. The printed 0.04=1/250 in Remark 2.3 is an arithmetic typo; our time units use 1/250 or 1/252 explicitly.

## Our replication status

No source Figure 2 replication claim. We derived exact constant-variance chi-square bias/variance and simulated independent finite-sampling error separately.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Include positive counterevidence to a noise-only narrative. Distinguish finite-M log bias, IQ/IV heterogeneity, serial dependence and price noise.
