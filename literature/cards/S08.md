# S08 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Mouti (2026), Rough Volatility Across Assets, arXiv 2608.16749v 1, 17 Aug 2026.

## Version and status

29 PDF pages, preprint under arXiv perpetual nonexclusive distribution license. This license does not license us to redistribute the PDF.

## Inspected source locations

PDF pages 7-9: observation corrections and fOU mean-reversion claim; p 10 Table 2; pp 11-13 simulation design/Table 3. pp 9-10 visually inspected.

## Model and assumptions

Stationary fOU log-variance for positive kappa; fBm case at kappa 0. Grid H={.05, .10, .15, .30, .50}, kappa={0, .003, .010, .020, .035}; daily dispersion calibrated to SPY.

## Observation and error layer

Daily latent variance drives 390 one-minute Gaussian return increments per day. Observed log-price adds Gaussian noise std 1e-4 and 5e-4; multiple RV estimators and an additive-log-error correction are compared.

## Estimator and target

OLS log second moment on lags 1..10 and 1..40; constant-offset correction and mean-reversion adjustments. The paper separates option-implied skew estimates from realized estimates.

## Data and horizon

Author reports 3926 US equities, 34 CME roots and options 44 underlyings over 2010-2025. These restricted underlying data were not accessed or independently validated.

## Source-grounded finding

The preprint reports persistent low corrected roughness estimates and gives an all-H fOU finite-lag contamination formula. Our Brownian special-case audit agrees with its Corollary 1.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Full fOU simulation initialization/parameter matching and error dependence require more audit. Numerical Table 2 check matched 35/48 cells within the prespecified 1e-4 tolerance, with maximum difference 0.00494. Independent spectral-variogram and oscillatory-covariance integrations agree in checked cells. Cause remains unresolved; do not label the paper invalid or silently replace its table.

## Our replication status

Secondary preprint formula audit: results/mouti_table 2_audit.csv and latest_mouti_audit.json. Not our required published-result replication; no Tables 3-6 Monte Carlo replication.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Treat cross-asset noise correction and mean reversion as existing work. A bounded methodological replication/extension is more defensible than claiming discovery of apparent roughness.
