# S05 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Bennedsen, Lunde & Pakkanen (2022), Decoupling the Short- and Long-Term Behavior of Stochastic Volatility, Journal of Financial Econometrics 20(5), 961-1006; DOI 10.1093/jjfinec/nbaa049.

## Version and status

Downloaded arXiv 1610.00332v 3, 5 Jan 2021 / cover 6 Jan 2021, 48 PDF pages. Published identity verified through author/institution records.

## Inspected source locations

PDF page 6 generalized Cauchy covariance; p 12 exact Gaussian simulation remark; pp 13-14 noisy variogram/NLLS; p 17 bandwidth/optimization sensitivity; pp 27, 37-38 empirical/forecasting scope. p 6 visually inspected.

## Model and assumptions

Generalized Cauchy rho(h)=(1+|h|^(2 alpha+1))^(-beta/(2 alpha+1)) and Brownian semistationary classes separate local alpha=H-1/2 from persistence.

## Observation and error layer

Additive iid independent log-volatility error gives observed variogram latent variogram+2 sigma_u^2. Exact power-law moment assumption underlies the stated NLLS consistency theorem.

## Estimator and target

OLS slope, and offset NLLS b 0+b 1 (kDelta)^(2 alpha+1). Their recommended practical averaging over bandwidths 10..20 is not used in our primary-lag 10 descriptive diagnostic; our variant is explicitly labeled.

## Data and horizon

E-mini S&P500 and a large TAQ equity panel; forecasting includes Cauchy and BSS alternatives. No restricted data from these samples were accessed.

## Source-grounded finding

Both roughness and persistence can matter to forecasting; neither a single H nor roughness alone parametrizes all relevant dependence. Nonlinear optimization and lag-band choices matter (p 17).

## Limitations and unresolved audit

The exact power-variogram theorem is not a consistency guarantee for fixed daily OU/Cauchy finite-lag fits. Cauchy Gaussianity limits extensions; integration changes the estimand.

## Our replication status

Source-grounded Cauchy simulator validated by PSD checks, exact embedding covariance and independent Cholesky draws. This is a new illustrative pilot, not a reproduction of their empirical tables.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Use a transparent stationary rough covariance with a separate persistence parameter and include finite OU mixtures. Do not rename Cauchy as fOU.
