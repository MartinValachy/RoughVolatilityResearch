# D05 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Boudt, Kleen & Sjoerup (2022), Analyzing Intraday Financial Data in R: The highfrequency Package, Journal of Statistical Software 104(8); DOI 10.18637/jss.v104.i08.

## Version and status

36 PDF-page OA article and highfrequency 1.0.2 repository commit eb 0e84898 ce 1c 44142726412529 bbcf 9e296a 066.

## Inspected source locations

PDF pages 23-26 SPYRM and HAR/HEAVY examples; p 24 code multipliesRVby 10000; maintainer SPYRM.Rd warns CLOSE is last recorded price.

## Model and assumptions

Documented realized-measure/forecasting software and example data.

## Observation and error layer

SPYRM has 1495 days, 2014-01-02 through 2019-12-31, 14 fields including 1/5 minute RV, RQ, BPV, median measures, kernels, CLOSE.

## Estimator and target

Our development pilot uses 750 rows through 2016; later 745 validation rows quarantined. No 2020-2025 rows acquired. Direct source-price recomputation is not possible from this daily-only file.

## Data and horizon

Small 134520 byteRDA, parsed by isolated pyreadr. Package GPL>=2; dataset upstream provenance/redistribution not separately established.

## Source-grounded finding

Audit found no duplicate dates and 756 expected XNYS sessions versus 750 present. All 6 missing dates are early-close sessions; RQ/RV^2 ratios suggest mixed units, but conversion is not source-verified.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Unknown exactsession M, timezone/source processing and RQconvention block empirical noise calibration/HARQ. CLOSE is unsuitable for an assumed official total-return series.

## Our replication status

Pilot dataset/data card/quality report produced. No market mechanism conclusion.

## Relevance and defensible extension

A real SPY data pilot with explicit field failures, keeping final historical test sealed.
