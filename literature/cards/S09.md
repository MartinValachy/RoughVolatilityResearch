# S09 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Shi, Yu & Zhang (2025), Realized Volatility Forecasting: Continuous versus Discrete Time Models, University of Macau Working Paper 202537, 21 Oct 2025.

## Version and status

43 PDF pages, official working-paper endpoint successfully downloaded after original 503/incomplete-response failures. Author page lists it as submitted.

## Inspected source locations

PDF pages 6-7 fGn likelihood; pp 19-20 HAR family; pp 21-22 projection/retransformation; pp 23-25 economic criterion/data; p 22 visually inspected.

## Model and assumptions

Continuous-time Gaussian fBm/fOU and BSS forecasting models compared with ARFIMA, HAR/HARQ and log variants.

## Observation and error layer

Forecasts of realized measures; HARQ explicitly uses quarticity. An explicit additional latent-log IV/proxy noise likelihood was not found in the inspected model/projection sections; a paper-wide absence claim is not made.

## Estimator and target

Time-domain and Whittle likelihoods, rolling 1255 day estimation, horizons 1/5/21. Eq 35 uses lognormal correction; forecast range filter falls back to training mean.

## Data and horizon

11 equity ETFs, 30 DJIAstocks and 30 crypto candidates; equities from Refinitiv Tick History at 5 minutes, 2010-2024; RTH9:30-16:00, exclude days with<50% expected 5 minute observations (p 25). Author-linked data ZIP returned 403 locally and was not accessed.

## Source-grounded finding

Economic forecasting overlap is substantial: risky-asset weight proportional to inverse forecast volatility and a risk-parity utility proxy with Sharpe 0.4, gamma 2 (pp 24-25). Statistical and economic rankings are explicitly separate.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Printed stationary conditional variance below eq 35 is gamma 0+Gamma^T Sigma^-1 Gamma. Gaussian conditioning requires a MINUS; our formula notebook/tests use the minus. The printed sign was visually checked, but author code is unavailable, so no claim is made that actual numerical results used it. Their utility proxy is not a transaction-cost/cash/overnight-accounted realized strategy return.

## Our replication status

Full text acquired and targeted equations audited. Official replication archive identified but 403 blocks access; no market forecast table replicated.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Do not claim first economic-value comparison of rough forecasts. Use aligned risk tracking, comparable observation treatment and executable accounting as a potential bounded extension.
