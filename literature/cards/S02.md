# S02 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Cont & Das (2024), Rough Volatility: Fact or Artefact?, Sankhya B; DOI 10.1007/s13571-024-00322-2.

## Version and status

Downloaded arXiv 2203.13820 author version (30 PDF pages). Published identity verified on Springer; author/journal equations not assumed byte-identical.

## Inspected source locations

PDF page 4: estimation-error argument; pp 5-7 Definitions 1-4, Theorems 2.1-2.2, statistic(5) and root(6); pp 12-14: spot, integrated and realized objects.

## Model and assumptions

A pathwise normalized p-variation method, distinct from a parametric fOU likelihood. Brownian and fractional processes provide simulation benchmarks.

## Observation and error layer

Realized volatility is the square root of sampled quadratic variation; estimation error from discretely sampled prices need not preserve Holder/variation regularity. The paper sometimes uses microstructure terminology broadly; this project reserves price noise for observed-price contamination.

## Estimator and target

Two nested partitions: block frequency K and finer frequency L. W(L, K, pi, p, T, X) sums coarse absolute p-increments divided by within-block fine absolute p-increments, times block length; solve W=T for p and report 1/p (p 7, eqs 5-6).

## Data and horizon

Controlled Brownian/fractional simulations and Oxford-Man realized signals; exact empirical exclusion/sampling grid not yet fully reconciled.

## Source-grounded finding

Their simulations show low apparent roughness for realized proxies even under Brownian spot volatility. The distinction follows from lack of regularity-norm convergence, not from failure of consistency for integrated variance (p 4).

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Pathwise asymptotics require refining partitions and assumptions on p-variation; finite L/K behavior matters. The conclusion is not a theorem that market volatility is nonrough. Our daily OLS diagnostic is not this estimator.

## Our replication status

Full text acquired; no normalized-variation replication implemented in this checkpoint.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Maintain separate implementations and estimands; prioritize validating a source-faithful estimator before any empirical latent H claim.
