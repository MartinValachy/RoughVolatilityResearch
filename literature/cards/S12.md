## Model and assumptions

Forecasts of a latent conditional variance evaluated by imperfect conditionally unbiased proxies; separates desired functional from arbitrary losses.

## Observation and error layer

Squared return, realized variance and range proxies with distribution-specific properties; robustness means preservation of expected forecast ranking under specified conditional mean assumptions.

## Estimator and target

Analytical expected-loss minimizers. QLIKE is log(f)+proxy/f, MSE is also robust under the stated assumptions. Losses on transformed targets need not be.

## Data and horizon

Our selected Table 1 is theoretical, standardized sigma^2=1 with t 6, t 10 and Gaussian returns; no market data needed.

## Source-grounded finding

Noisy-proxy loss choice can change optimal forecasts even when variance is fixed. This directly affects interpretation of an economic comparison.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Conditional unbiasedness is essential. A robust ranking does not prove structural identification, and a proxy-based utility criterion targets a different objective.
