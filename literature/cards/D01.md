## Citation

Cipollini, Cruciani, Gallo, Insana, Otranto & Spagnolo (2026), VOLatility Archive for Realized Estimates (VOLARE), arXiv 2602.19732.

## Version and status

48 PDF-page infrastructure paper; live frontend snapshot separately hashed. The paper inventory is historical documentation, not verified live availability.

## Inspected source locations

PDF page 13 stock session rules; pp 18-23 realized measures; AppendixA security inventory. Live JS snapshot shows login-protected download flows.

## Model and assumptions

A data-processing infrastructure for stocks, FX, futures, not a latent roughness model.

## Observation and error layer

Kibot tick data; stocks extend collection to 16:05 ET to capture delayed close prints, while early-close observations after 13:00 are excluded (p 13). Documentation says no raw tick redistribution through platform.

## Estimator and target

Daily realized measures include RV/RQ/bipower/kernels at documented grids. Exact live count and downloadable field inventory cannot be verified without access.

## Data and horizon

Paper lists historical security universe and dates. Current public homepage/documentation are React shells; frontend requires a token/login for data. SPY availability is not claimed.

## Source-grounded finding

Provider session conventions differ from a naive 9:30-16:00 assumption; these must be mapped before comparison.

No account or existing entitlement verified; no credentials used or access restrictions bypassed. Licensing and live asset availability remain unresolved.
