# S13 - targeted evidence card

This is a targeted methodological audit, not a claim that every source section has been fully checked.

## Citation

Andersen, Bollerslev & Meddahi (2011), Realized volatility forecasting and market microstructure noise, Journal of Econometrics 160(1), 220-234; DOI 10.1016/j.jeconom.2010.03.032.

## Version and status

15 PDF-page journal article from coauthor Duke page.

## Inspected source locations

PDF pages 2-3 /journal 221-222: ESV setup, price observation eq 2.6 and iid noise assumptions; integrated sum target eq 2.3.

## Model and assumptions

Eigenfunction stochastic-volatility diffusions allow analytic linear forecasts and compare alternative realized measures.

## Observation and error layer

Observed log price equals efficient log price+iid independent centered price noise with finite fourth moment. Noise differencing yields MA1 return errors, distinct from daily additive log RV error.

## Estimator and target

Moment-based optimal linear forecasts of future integrated volatility, with realized-estimator and sampling-frequency comparison.

## Data and horizon

Analytic theory, simulations/illustrations; original market sample not fully audited here.

## Source-grounded finding

Explicit price noise can materially change realized measures and forecast performance; optimal measurement and optimal forecasting are related but distinct.

## Limitations and unresolved audit

Simple iid price noise and independent return/volatility drivers limit direct empirical transfer. More frequent sampling need not improve ordinaryRV.

## Our replication status

No source table reproduced. Level B price-noise demonstration is independently implemented and labeled illustrative.

## Relevance and defensible extension

Keep price-noise bias separate from efficient-price finite-sampling uncertainty.
