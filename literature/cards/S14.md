## Model and assumptions

Efficient-price quadratic variation observed with market frictions; realized kernel corrects realized variance using symmetric lagged return autocovariances.

## Observation and error layer

Separate price-noise layer. The paper treats simple independent frictions and extensions to temporal dependence/endogeneity; assumptions depend on the selected theorem.

## Estimator and target

Kernel RV+weighted positive/negative realized autocovariances. Bandwidth/kernel smoothness determine rates and asymptotic variance; this is not ordinaryRV with a quarticity plug-in.

## Data and horizon

Simulation and NYSE illustration described in the introduction; exact empirical inputs not audited.

## Source-grounded finding

A robust realized measure requires explicit noise/kernel/boundary conventions. It does not become ground truth merely because it is noise robust.
